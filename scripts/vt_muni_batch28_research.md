## Charters

- **Windsor — ch. 173**: §12 SB of 5 elected (three 3-yr, two 2-yr); §15
  Moderator elected 1-yr; §16 Town Clerk elected 3-yr; §17 Town Treasurer
  elected 3-yr (also collector of current and delinquent taxes ex officio);
  §29(d) Fence Viewer, Grand Juror, Inspector of Lumber, Weigher of Coal,
  Second Constable, and **Auditor are abolished**; §29(a)(7) Town Assessor
  appointed ("may be a full-time lister") — no lister post; §29(a)(9) a
  Constable MAY be appointed — permissive, post only if a holder is
  published.
- **Winooski — ch. 19**: §301 Council = Mayor + 4 Councilors at large
  (councilors 2-yr staggered); §303 Mayor elected 3-yr; §501 City Manager
  appointed; §506(b) all other city officers appointed by the City Manager;
  §506(c) appointive list: City Clerk, City Grand Juror, Constable,
  Collector of Taxes, Police Chief, Fire Chief, Fire Marshal, City
  Assessor, Zoning Administrator, Emergency Management Coordinator, Human
  Resources Director — **no Treasurer in the charter list** (Fenney's city
  role to be settled from the city's own site).
- **Woodford — ch. 175**: §8 elected officers exhaustive: Moderator (floor,
  1-yr), Selectboard 3 (3-yr staggered), Listers 3 (3-yr staggered), Town
  Clerk 3-yr, Town Treasurer 3-yr (collector of current taxes ex officio),
  Delinquent Tax Collector 3-yr, Trustees of Public Funds 4 **ex officio**
  (the three SB members + the Treasurer — no separate TPF post, New Haven
  precedent), Auditors 3 (3-yr staggered). §11 cemetery commissioners are
  appointed. No constable/grand juror/town agent posts.
- **Woodstock Village — ch. 291**: §3 annual meeting elects a Clerk, **five
  Trustees**, a Treasurer, and a Collector of Taxes. §9 fire wardens may be
  elected pursuant to bylaws — permissive, no number fixed; joins the
  fire-officer scope question absent evidence of current practice.
- Winhall, Wolcott, Woodbury, Woodstock (town), Worcester: no charter
  chapter — general law, 17 V.S.A. §2646.
