## Charters — officer structure (read firsthand)
- Barre City ch. 001 s.205: voters elect biennially a MAYOR and a
  CLERK; each of 3 wards elects ONE councilor annually to a 2-year
  term => council of 6 (2 per ward, staggered). Treasurer is NOT
  an elected office (s.205 list is closed) => NO treasurer post
  (Metivier's treasurer role is appointed staff-side). Posts:
  mayor (1), councilor-ward-1/2/3 (2 each, shared City Council
  org), city clerk (1), JP (15).
- Barre Town ch. 101 s.10: elected = 5 Selectboard, one Clerk,
  one Moderator. Treasurer item REPEALED => NO treasurer post.
  No elected listers/auditors/etc. Posts: SB (5), clerk (1),
  moderator (1), JP (15).
- Bennington ch. 103 s.201: elective = 7 Select Board (at large),
  Town Clerk, Treasurer, Moderator (3-year terms). s.202: Constable
  and Listers APPOINTED by the board => no lister/constable posts.
  Town Manager performs the delinquent-tax collector duties
  (s.404(a)(9)); Select Board discharges Town Agent duties
  (s.303(7)); CPA audit (s.303(5)). Posts: SB (7), clerk (1),
  treasurer (1), moderator (1), JP (15).
- Bellows Falls ch. 207 s.5: officers = Moderator, PRESIDENT,
  Clerk, Treasurer, Collector of Taxes, FOUR Trustees, THREE
  Auditors of Accounts — all elected at the annual meeting
  EXCEPT the Collector of Taxes (appointed by the trustees).
  President is a Board member ex officio and presides (s.7) =>
  board = President + 4 Trustees (factory's "5 trustees" counts
  the President). Posts: president (1), trustee (4) [shared Board
  org], village clerk (1, elected), village treasurer (1,
  elected), moderator (1), auditor (3), collector-of-taxes (1,
  appointed; holder if the village site names one).
- No charter chapters: Barnard, Barnet, Barton T, Barton V,
  Belvidere, Benson (general law).

## Modeling rule settled this batch
Posts exist where the municipality's constitutive law establishes
the office (17 V.S.A. s.2646 for general-law towns; the charter's
own officer list for chartered municipalities). how_seated then
records election vs appointment for the current holder (batch-1
precedents: Alburgh appointed treasurer; Alburgh Village's
charter-appointed clerk/treasurer). A staff role with no
constitutive office (Barre City/Town treasurer) gets no post.
