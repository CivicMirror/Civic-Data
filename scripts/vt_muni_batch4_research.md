## Charter checks (Title 24 Appendix, read firsthand 2026-10-18)

- **Bridport — ch. 107A** (§§1–4, added 2015 No. M-16): §2 general law applies except as charter provides. **§3: Town Treasurer AND Town Clerk are APPOINTED by the Selectboard** (neither may hold an elective town office simultaneously; residency not required). → Berlin/Alburgh-Village corollary: clerk + treasurer posts created, holders how_seated "appointed" (Guide: Julie Howlett, Clerk; Kathleen Shimel, Treasurer). Appointed Treasurer collects CURRENT taxes (§3(a)(1)(B)); DTC stays general-law (post only if a source verifies separate fill).
- **Bristol — ch. 108** (merger charter, 1994 No. M-20, eff. Jan 1, 1995; Village of Bristol merged into the town — no separate Bristol village in repo ✓): §201 general law applies; **§301: officers as provided by general law, with the Selectboard fixed at five** (two added seats). Selectboard sits as water/sewer commissioners (§§501(d), 601(b)) — no separate elected utility body. Police is a special service district with no separate elected officers (§701). → Bristol = general-law §2646 office set, SB seats 5. Clerk + Treasurer separate elected posts even though one person (Sharon Lucia) holds both (Barton/Belvidere precedent).
- **Burlington — ch. 003** (full city charter): ELECTED set = **Mayor** (§3(a)/§125(a), 3-yr term, first Tuesday in March triennially), **City Councilors** (§3(b)/§125(b): 4 district + 8 ward, all 2-yr), **School Commissioners** (§4, 12 — DEFERRED to school pass), **Ward Clerks** (§10(a)–(b): 1 per ward, 2-yr) and **Inspectors of Election** (§10: 3 per ward, 3-yr staggered) — both elected by the legal voters of each ward. §5: all these elections by ranked-choice ballot. APPOINTIVE (no posts except as noted): **Chief Administrative Officer** (§122(a) mayoral appointment w/ Council approval; §138 — the CAO performs the town-clerk duties and holds town-treasurer powers; the charter has NO elected clerk or treasurer) → CAO post created under the Berlin corollary (charter-established appointive office), holder Katherine Schad how_seated "appointed" (city site title check pending). **City Constable** (§122(a) appointed; §152 — 2nd/3rd/4th constables "in case such are appointed") → constable post, holder if a city source names one, appointed. **City Grand Juror** (§122(a) appointed; §151 — plus an Assistant City Grand Juror, also appointed) → grand juror post, holder if named, appointed. ALL §120 boards/commissions (Assessors, Tax Appeals, Cemetery, Light, Fire, Health, Airport, Police, Parks & Rec, Library, Planning, Public Works) are appointed by the City Council with Mayor presiding → NO posts. CAO collects delinquent taxes (§108) → NO separate DTC post. NO elected lister/auditor/moderator offices.
- **Brighton, Brookfield, Brookline, Brownington, Brunswick, Burke, Cabot**: NO chapter in Title 24 Appendix — general law (17 V.S.A. §2646 set).
