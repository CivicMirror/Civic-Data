## Charters (read firsthand 2026-10-19)
- Plainfield T — ch. 131: §401 officers are general-law except
  as varied; §402 elected by Australian ballot; §702 water and
  sewer commissioners are APPOINTED by the Selectboard (3) — no
  posts.
- Poultney T — ch. 133: §303 Selectboard = 3 members on
  staggered 3-yr terms + 2 members on 1-yr terms (matches the
  directory's term labels). §304: Town Clerk 3-yr; Town
  Treasurer 3-yr; three Auditors, 3-yr staggered; Library
  Trustees 5 on 5-yr terms, one elected each year (§§302(1),
  304(c)). §302(2): water and sewer commissioners elected
  "(in the event the Selectboard so directs)" — whether the
  office is exercised is a town-practice question; the charter
  states no ratepayer franchise (town-meeting election). §902:
  Fire Chief/assistants elected by the Hose Company membership
  — internal company election, NOT modeled and not part of the
  public fire-officers question.
- Poultney V — ch. 269: §303 five trustees, two elected each
  year to 2-yr terms; §302(a) elective officers: trustees,
  Village Clerk/Treasurer (combined office, 1-yr term,
  §304(a)), Village President (elected at the annual meeting
  from among the trustees, §303(b); the President serves as
  Village Moderator, §302(d) — no separate moderator post), one
  auditor each year (three auditors, 3-yr staggered, §304(b)),
  and water and sewer commissioners if the trustees so direct.
  §702: Tax Collector is the Village Manager ex officio
  (appointed) — no collector post.
- Peacham, Peru, Pittsfield, Pittsford, Plymouth, Pomfret,
  Pownal: no Title 24 Appendix chapter → general law.
