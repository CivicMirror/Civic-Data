## Charters — Title 24 Appendix (read firsthand)
Chapters exist: Berlin ch. 105, Bradford ch. 106, Brandon
ch. 106B, Brattleboro ch. 107. NONE for Berkshire, Bethel,
Braintree, Bridgewater (general law). Bolton has NO town
charter chapter (only Bolton Fire District No. 1, ch. 504 —
a fire district, not the town).
- Brandon ch. 106B: only s.1 (general-law application) + s.2
  (local option tax). No officer provisions -> Brandon uses
  the general-law 17 V.S.A. s.2646 officer set.
- Berlin ch. 105 (https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/fullchapter/24APPENDIX/105):
  s.41 officers are those provided by law except as the
  charter otherwise provides. s.42 ELECTED: (1) Moderator,
  from the floor, 1-year; (3) Selectboard 5 (3 staggered
  3-year + 2 one-year terms); (4) "such other Town officers
  as are provided by law." s.44(b) APPOINTED by the
  Selectboard: Town Administrator, Assistant Town
  Administrator, Treasurer, Collector of Delinquent Taxes,
  Road Superintendent, Police Chief, Zoning Administrator,
  Assistant Zoning Administrator, Assessor or assessing
  services, Town Clerk. s.44(c): Grand Juror duties assigned
  to the Police Chief -> NO grand juror post. Berlin modeling:
  SB (5, filled) + Moderator elected; Clerk / Treasurer / DTC
  are charter-established APPOINTIVE offices -> posts exist,
  holders minted how_seated "appointed" if an official source
  names them (Alburgh V precedent); NO lister post (assessor
  appointed); Auditors / Constables / Town Agent unmentioned
  -> remain s.2646-elected via s.41.
- Bradford ch. 106 (https://legislature.vermont.gov/statutes/fullchapter/24APPENDIX/106):
  the 2004 Town+Village of Bradford MERGER charter (village
  merged into the town Nov 30, 2004; the repo correctly has
  no separate Bradford village). s.202: "The Town of Bradford
  shall have the officers as provided for towns by the
  general laws" -> the general-law s.2646 set governs.
  s.206a creates a Board of Water and Sewer Commissioners —
  5 persons, ELECTED by Bradford resident ratepayers at
  annual town meeting, 5-year terms. OPEN MODELING
  QUESTION — do NOT model unilaterally: a ratepayer-franchise
  body sits next to Walter's restricted-franchise scope rule;
  flag on #163 in the batch report for his ruling.
- Brattleboro ch. 107 (index + s.2.2 + s.2.7 + s.4.6 read):
  s.2.2 ELECTED by Australian ballot on the first Tuesday in
  March: (1) Representative Town Meeting members, by
  district, body not to exceed 140 members; (2) Selectboard
  of 5 at large (2 one-year + 3 three-year terms); (5) Board
  of three listers at large, one elected each year, 3-year
  terms; (6) three trustees of public funds at large, one
  each year, 3-year terms; (7) Moderator, First Constable,
  Second Constable (1-year terms). Items (3),(4) [the former
  elected Town Clerk and Town Treasurer] REPEALED (2011
  No. M-10, eff. April 27, 2012) -> Brattleboro clerk and
  treasurer are appointed staff, NOT town offices under the
  charter -> NO clerk/treasurer posts (Barre City rule;
  contrast Berlin, where ch. 105 s.44(b) expressly
  constitutes them appointed town officers). s.2.7: the Board
  of Library Trustees (9) is elected BY the Representative
  Town Meeting from nominees, not by the voters -> indirect
  selection, outside the elected-officer sweep, no post.
  s.4.6(20): the Selectboard provides "for all other services
  that would otherwise be performed by an elected auditor"
  -> NO elected auditor post. The Representative Town Meeting
  itself (up to 140 district-elected members) is NOT built in
  this batch — flagged on #163 as a scope/follow-up question
  for Walter (it is the town's legislative body under the
  charter; a district roster pass of its own).
