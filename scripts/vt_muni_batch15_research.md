## Charters read FIRSTHAND (2026-10-18)
- **Milton ch. 129**: §202(a) ELECTED at annual meeting:
  five selectpersons, Town Clerk (3-yr, §203(b)), Town
  Treasurer (3-yr, §203(c)), five Library Trustees (§203(d)),
  five School Trustees (school pass), Champlain Water
  District Representative (3-yr, §203(f)). §306 APPOINTED by
  the Selectboard: Moderator (§306(e)(6)), Town Assessor
  (§306(e)(7) — no listers), and permissively a Constable
  (§306(b)(4)) and a Collector of Taxes (§306(b)(2)) — posts
  only if the town publishes holders. Auditors: none — the
  charter provides for audit by a CPA (§304 area) → no post.
  The §202/§306 lists are the modeling authority.
- **Montgomery ch. 129B** (2021): purpose, general law,
  local-options tax, separability — NO officer provisions →
  general law (§2646).
- **Morristown ch. 129C** (2025, No. M-2): corporate
  existence, general law, Town Manager — NO officer
  provisions → general law (§2646).
- **Montpelier ch. 005**: §302 Council = Mayor + 2 members
  from each of 3 voting districts (2-yr, one elected per
  district per year); §401 Mayor elected citywide (2-yr);
  §509(a) elected at annual meeting: Mayor, City Clerk
  (3-yr), one Council member per district, "other elective
  City officers," school directors (school pass). §906 City
  Treasurer APPOINTED by the Council → post, appointed
  holder. City Manager appointed by the Council — staff, no
  post. Modeling follows Barre City: per-district councilor
  posts (2 seats each).
- **Morrisville Village ch. 245**: §6(a) officers = a
  Moderator, five Trustees (5-yr terms, one elected each
  year), a Clerk, and a Treasurer — ALL ELECTED at the
  annual meeting (second Monday in April, §13). §7(g)
  trustees elect a Chairperson from themselves (no president
  post). No auditors in the §6 list → no post.
- Monkton, Moretown, Morgan, Mount Holly, Mount Tabor: no
  chapter in the Title 24 Appendix index → general law.
