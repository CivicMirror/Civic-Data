## Charters read firsthand (2026-10-19)

- **North Troy — ch. 253 §5:** at every annual meeting the
  electors choose a Moderator, a Clerk, a Treasurer, a
  Collector of Taxes, three trustees (staggered 3-year terms,
  one per year), **three fire wardens**, and an **Attorney**.
  §20: the Village Constable is **appointed** by the trustees —
  no post. Annual meeting in January. The elected fire wardens
  and the elected "Attorney" are flagged in the report.
- **Northfield (town) — ch. 129D:** §301 general-law officers
  except as the charter varies; §302 five-member Selectboard;
  §201 town-manager form. **§303 Water/Wastewater Commissioners
  and §304 Electric Utility Commissioners each seat two members
  "elected by the ratepayers" of the systems (third seat an
  appointed selectboard member) — a restricted franchise, OUT
  of scope under the 2026-10-15 rule; no posts, flagged.**
- **Orleans — ch. 261 §8:** officers are a Moderator, Clerk,
  three trustees (ballot; staggered 3-year terms, one per
  year), Treasurer, Collector of Taxes, a **Chief Engineer and
  two assistant engineers** — all but trustees elected annually
  (1-year terms). §§14–15 give auditors duties but §8 does not
  list them; practice decides. The elected engineers join the
  fire-officers scope question (Jacksonville pattern).
- **Panton — ch. 129F** is a recall provision only → general
  law (§2646).
- **Norton, Norwich, Old Bennington, Orange, Orwell:** no
  Title 24 Appendix chapter → general law. (A Village of
  Northfield charter, ch. 257, exists on the books but no such
  jurisdiction is in the 278-municipality universe.)
