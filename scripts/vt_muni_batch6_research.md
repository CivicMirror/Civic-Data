## Charters (Title 24 Appendix index + chapters read firsthand, Oct 18, 2026)
- **Colchester ch. 113** — full charter. §201 officers per general law
  except as the charter provides. **§202 ELECTED** (Australian ballot):
  Selectboard 5 (three 3-yr + two 2-yr; one of each elected yearly);
  Moderator; **one Lister yearly (3-yr term → board of 3)**; **one
  Library Trustee yearly (5-yr term → board of 5)**. §202(b) 40%
  runoff rule. **Clerk + Treasurer are APPOINTED** — §404(k) the Town
  Manager appoints them (SB advice/consent as department heads);
  §801(a) all non-elected employees are manager-appointed. §501: the
  three elected listers sit as the Board of Assessment. §702(c):
  Treasurer holds general-law collection powers (tax sales) → no
  separate DTC office. §211(b)(5) CPA audit → no elected auditors.
  Cemeteries are manager-run (§404(n)) with an SB-appointed Cemetery
  Advisory Committee (§208(a)(3)) → no cemetery post. NO constable /
  DTC / GJ / TA / TPF in the charter → no posts. Modeling: SB,
  Moderator, Lister (3), Library Trustee (5) elected posts; Clerk +
  Treasurer posts with appointed holders (Berlin ch. 105 §44(b)
  precedent); JPs as usual.
- **Danville ch. 114 §101** — Australian ballot for budget votes only;
  no officer provisions → general law.
- **No chapter**: Chittenden, Clarendon, Concord, Corinth, Cornwall,
  Coventry, Craftsbury, Danby → general law (§2646 set).
