## Charters read FIRSTHAND (2026-10-18)
- **Middlebury ch. 127**: §301 officers are those provided by law
  except as the charter provides. §302(a) ELECTED at annual
  meeting: 5 Selectboard members (+2 more if the town votes
  under §302(e) — Middlebury carries 7 per the directory and
  the factory, so the expansion was voted; SB post = 7 seats),
  Town Meeting Moderator (annual, §304(c)), 5 Trustees of the
  Ilsley Library (§304(e)), 3 Listers (§304(a)), 3 Auditors
  unless eliminated under §2651b (§304(b) — elimination status
  to check in town sources), Town Clerk (3-yr, §304(d));
  §302(a)(7) repealed; school officers in §302(a)(8)–(9) are
  the school pass, not modeled. §305 + §306(d)(7): Treasurer
  APPOINTED by the Selectboard → post, appointed holder
  (Elizabeth "Beth" Dow). §306(d) Selectboard also appoints:
  First Constable (+ Second if needed), grand juror(s),
  Collector of Taxes, Town Agent → posts with appointed
  holders where published, else structure-only; seat counts
  for grand juror/constable are appointive discretions, so
  those posts are created only if town sources fix them —
  else no post (constable seat-count rule).
- **Manchester Village ch. 239** (1943, No. 183): §6 ELECTED at
  the annual meeting (second Monday of July): a President
  (1-yr; presides as moderator AND is ex officio one of the
  trustees and President of the Board), a Clerk (1-yr), a
  Treasurer (1-yr), a Tax Collector (1-yr), 3 Auditors (1-yr),
  and 2 Trustees elected each year for 2-yr terms → Board of
  Trustees = 4 elected trustees + the President ex officio =
  5 seats. Modeling: President post (1 seat); Trustee post
  (4 seats); Village Clerk / Treasurer / Tax Collector /
  Auditors posts per §6. §9: Chief Engineer + assistant
  engineers are APPOINTED by the trustees → NOT modeled, and
  because they are appointive here they do NOT join the
  elected fire-officers scope question.
