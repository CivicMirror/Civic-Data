## Charters (read firsthand 2026-10-19)

- Readsboro ch. 141: Town/Village merger charter. §21 the
  Selectboard is the legislative body; no officer variations
  → general law governs the officer set.
- Richford ch. 142: Town/Village merger charter (village
  abolished eff. Jan 1, 1999). §202(a) officers are those
  provided by law for towns. §108 the Selectboard reverts to
  "the statutory limit" (5). **§203: the Town Treasurer
  collects taxes; "The Town shall not elect other collectors
  of current or delinquent taxes" → NO DTC post.**
- Richmond ch. 143: §302(c) — the Town **shall not have**
  Road Commissioner, **Town Agent, Town Auditor, or Town
  Grand Juror** → no posts for those three. §303(b) the
  Collector of Delinquent Taxes is **appointed** by the
  Selectboard after each annual meeting → DTC post,
  appointed holder. §902 Water and Sewer Commissioners are
  **appointed** by the Selectboard → no posts, and they do
  not join the Bradford ratepayer-election scope question.
  §501 SB: five members, one 3-year + one 2-year seat
  elected each year (staggered).
- Proctor, Putney, Randolph, Reading, Ripton, Rochester,
  Rockingham: no Title 24 Appendix chapter → general law.
