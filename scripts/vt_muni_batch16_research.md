## Charters read FIRSTHAND (Title 24 Appendix; local pulls `charter_{007,247,249,251}.txt`)

- **Newport City ch. 007**: §6 — elected at the annual meeting: Mayor,
  City Clerk, **four Aldermen**, City Treasurer, **one Constable, three
  Listers, three Auditors, and three Grand Jurors** (1-yr terms unless
  otherwise provided). §7 — Mayor + Board of Aldermen constitute the
  City Council; the aldermen elect one of their number President. §8 —
  the Council APPOINTS the Collector of Taxes (and other officers).
  §16 — a Department of Assessment (Chief Assessor appointed by the
  Council) exists alongside the elected listers.
- **Newbury Village ch. 247**: §401 — officers = a Moderator, **three
  Trustees**, a Clerk, a Treasurer, and **three Auditors**, all elected
  at the annual meeting (1-yr terms; trustees excepted — see the chapter
  text for their stagger). §403 — trustees fill vacancies by appointment
  until an election. §701 — annual meeting the fourth Tuesday of May.
  (This is the chapter a batch-14 search snippet had misattributed to
  Marshfield Village; verified firsthand here as Newbury's.)
- **Newfane Village ch. 249**: §201 — officers = **three Trustees, a
  Clerk, a Treasurer, a Collector of Taxes, an Auditor, and a Fire
  Marshal**, all chosen by ballot at each annual meeting. §305 — the
  Collector of Taxes is ex officio Chief of Police. §601 — annual
  meeting the first Wednesday in May.
- **North Bennington ch. 251**: §203(a) — at every annual meeting elect
  a Moderator, Clerk, Treasurer, **three Auditors**, and the trustees
  needed to fill vacancies; §301 — trustees serve staggered 3-yr terms,
  the other officers 1-yr. §401 — the trustees APPOINT the Fire
  Department's Chief and two assistant engineers (appointed, NOT
  elected — Manchester Village pattern; they do not join the
  fire-officers scope question).
- New Haven, Newark, Newbury (town), Newfane (town), Newport (town),
  North Hero: no chapter in the Title 24 Appendix index → general law
  (17 V.S.A. §2646).
