## Charters — FIRSTHAND reads of the local full-chapter pulls
(~/workspace/vt163_sources/charter_<ch>.txt)
- Elmore ch. 114G: local-option tax for capital projects ONLY →
  no officer provisions; general law.
- Enosburgh ch. 115 §101: highway-budget article ONLY → no
  officer provisions; general law.
- Essex ch. 117 §201: "The elected officers of the Town of Essex
  shall be Selectboard members and Moderator." §202: SB of 5,
  3-yr terms, at large. Town Manager appointed by the SB → Clerk
  and Treasurer are appointed offices under the manager system
  (posts with appointed holders per the modeling rule; NO
  lister/auditor/constable/DTC/GJ/TA/TPF posts — §201's elected
  list is exhaustive). JPs elected under general law.
- Essex Junction ch. 004 (Act M-10 of 2021, eff. July 1, 2022):
  elective City Council of 5 (§§202-204; the Council elects its
  own President/Vice President/Clerk from its members). §301:
  Brownell Library Trustees — 5 members, ELECTED, 5-yr terms.
  §302: Moderator APPOINTED by the Council (1-yr). City Manager
  appointed by the Council (§601); the Manager recommends the
  City Clerk's appointment annually with Council approval
  (§602(o)) → Clerk APPOINTED; a Treasurer exists as a
  Manager-appointed employee (§602) → post only if the city names
  a holder. No other elected offices; the old Village of Essex
  Junction charter (ch. 221) is REPEALED. City JPs elected (SOS
  listing has an Essex Junction City block of 15).
- Enosburg Falls ch. 219: §3(a) annual meeting elects by ballot,
  each for one year: a Moderator, a Clerk, a Treasurer. §3(b) the
  village may elect such other officers as the public interest
  requires. §4: Board of FIVE Trustees, staggered 3-yr terms
  (§4(b)) — factory's 5 trustees fit the charter. §3(c) trustee-
  filled vacancies by appointment.
