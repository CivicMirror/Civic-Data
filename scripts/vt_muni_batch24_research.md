## Charters (read firsthand from legislature.vermont.gov,
## saved b24_charter_015/155/157.txt)
Vergennes ch. 15: sec. 4 ELECTS a Mayor (2-yr), six
aldermen (three elected annually to 2-yr terms), a lister
(3-yr staggered), one Auditor (3-yr), and a City Grand
Juror; the Mayor presides with the power of moderator
(sec. 6 -- no separate moderator post). Sec. 9: the City
Council APPOINTS the City Manager, the Treasurer, the City
Clerk, and one or more constables -- so Betsy Sullivan is
an APPOINTEE in both her offices (St. Albans City shape).
Underhill ch. 155: general law except sec. 22 -- "The Town
shall not elect other collectors of current or delinquent
taxes" (the Treasurer collects) -- so NO DTC post; the
charter presupposes elected auditors (secs. 41-42) and
changes nothing else in the office set.
Waitsfield ch. 157: sec. 4 elected officers are those
required by state law (Australian ballot), BUT sec. 5(a)
the Selectboard APPOINTS the Town Clerk and the Town
Treasurer (1-3 yr terms) -- Peterson and Lewis are
appointees; the rest of the sec. 2646 roster is elected.
