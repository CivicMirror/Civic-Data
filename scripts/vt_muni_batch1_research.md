## Alburgh Village charter — 24 App. V.S.A. ch. 203 (read in full)
- s.301(a): elected at each annual meeting (first Tuesday in May):
  ONE trustee (5-year terms -> board of 5, staggered), ONE Collector
  (1-year), ONE auditor (3-year terms -> 3 seats, staggered).
- s.301(c): Village Clerk and Village Treasurer are APPOINTED by the
  Board of Trustees. -> Cheryl Dunn's two memberships: how_seated
  "appointed".
- s.303: trustees fill vacancies by appointment (fits the Aug 5, 2026
  vacancy notice + the factory's appointed-Vincelette note).
Village posts: trustee (5, filled from the village site), village
clerk (1, Dunn, appointed), village treasurer (1, Dunn, appointed),
collector (1, STRUCTURE ONLY - charter s.301(a)(2), no holder
